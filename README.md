# Macroeconometrics-Okuns-Law-Analysis
## Overview
This project studies the empirical relationship between economic growth and unemployment through Okun’s Law.

The analysis investigates how GDP growth affects labor market dynamics and unemployment variations.

## Objectives
- Test the validity of Okun’s Law
- Analyze the relationship between growth and unemployment
- Study labor market adjustment mechanisms
- Compare theoretical and empirical results

## Theoretical Background
The project is based on Arthur Okun’s (1962) framework linking:
- economic growth
- labor productivity
- unemployment dynamics

The study also discusses:
- labor force participation effects
- delayed hiring adjustments
- cyclical labor market responses

## Methodology
- Macroeconomic time series analysis
- Econometric estimation
- Correlation analysis
- Regression modeling
- Empirical interpretation

## Key Insights
- GDP growth does not reduce unemployment proportionally
- Productivity dynamics affect employment adjustments
- Labor market rigidities slow unemployment reductions
- Okun’s relationship varies across economic cycles
